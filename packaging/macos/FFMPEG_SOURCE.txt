The build reports "--enable-gpl --enable-version3" and includes libx264 among other
libraries, so the binaries are distributed under the GNU GPL version 3 (licenses/ffmpeg/GPL-3.0.txt).
The unmodified ffmpeg and ffprobe are redistributed as separate programs invoked by Birdcage.

These are Intel (x86_64) binaries. On Apple Silicon they run through Rosetta 2. If a native
ffmpeg/ffprobe is installed (for example Homebrew in /opt/homebrew/bin), Birdcage uses that first.
